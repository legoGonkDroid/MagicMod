# MagicMod 0.1.0

First public version.

- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.267
- 106 mods centred on Ars Nouveau, Cataclysm, MineColonies and Relics
- FTB Quests book with 16 chapters: *The Journey* (novice → Ender Dragon) and *Codex & Collections*
- KubeJS tweaks: sneak + right-click with an empty hand opens the Armor Statues menu; the vanilla recipe book is hidden outside creative (use EMI)
- Starter Kit "Novice Mage": spell book, tier 1 Arcanist armour, Structurize build tool, Supply Camp and Town Hall
- Datapack `archwood_village_waystones` (add it per world) puts waystones in Archwood villages
